During my undergraduate studies, I completed my second internship, a 16-month placement that gave me the unique opportunity to gain in-depth technical experience. This role was my introduction to communication systems and radio-frequency (RF) engineering. I contributed to the design verification of RF circuits, implemented hardware modifications to improve digital and RF performance, and developed scripts to automate hardware testing. I also gained exposure to the full product lifecycle, from research and development and prototyping to pre-qualification testing and production.
